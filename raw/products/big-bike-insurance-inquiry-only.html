Big Bike Insurance

Ride with peace of mind! When you’re on the road, accidents may happen anytime, anywhere. This is why it’s important that you and your big bike has maximum protection coverage with Trinity Big Bike Insurance.

Trinity Big Bike Insurance is a comprehensive insurance that provides full protection for all damages and losses to the insured vehicle, including liabilities that may be incurred for its use.

COVERAGE:

  • Own Damage and Theft *Insured Value is based on Fair Market Value or Sales Invoice (for brand new)
  • Acts of Nature
  • Riot, Strike and Civil Commotion
  • Third Party Liability for Bodily Injury – PHP200,000.00
  • Third Party Liability for Property Damage – PHP200,000.00

 

EXTENSIONS:

  • Personal Accident Insurance
    • Accidental Death & Disablement : Php 100,000 each for 2 persons
    • Medical Reimbursement : Php 10,000 per person
    • Burial Benefit : Php 10,000 per person
  • “24/7 Hassle-Free” emergency roadside assistance, subject to the following benefits (Applicable only to motorcycles with 400cc displacement or greater):
    • Vehicle Assistance
    • Towing Assistance – up to P5,000
    • Personal Assistance
    • Protection Assistance

 

INSURING CONDITIONS:

  • Sum Insured of all brand-new motorcycles shall be based on the dealer’s selling price, including built-in or standard accessories only and shall NOT be reduced by any discounts.
  • This program shall NOT be applicable for vehicles with tax exemptions
  • Warranted usage of helmet and safety gear
  • Non-Casa repairs for units more than 5 years old, including Third Party Property Damage Claims

 

REFERRAL PROGRAM:

  • Get 10% Discount from Own Damage & Theft Premium Rate
  • Discounts applicable per single issued policy of the referrer
