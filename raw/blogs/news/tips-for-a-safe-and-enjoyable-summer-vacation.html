Tips for a Safe and Enjoyable Summer Vacation

April 4, 2023

Summer is finally here. It's a good time because the kids are out of school, the weather is pleasant, and we're inspired to have some fun and let loose. Many of us enjoy spending the summer planning vacations and visiting exciting destinations with friends and family. While the prospect of visiting a new location is exciting, it can also be a stressful activity. The stress of meticulously planning the perfect vacation can be taxing and weigh heavily on our lives. We even forget that the entire purpose of vacation is to relax and have fun. That being said, we've compiled a list of tips to keep in mind when planning your summer vacation and how to make it the most stress-free and enjoyable part of your summer.

PREPARATION TIPS

  1. Do your research – Prior research can provide you with deep insights into the area, telling you what to avoid and where to go, as well as an endless amount of additional information that will aid your journey.
  2. Book Early – Travel is on the rise, and waiting until the last minute only results in higher prices, especially for flights and hotels during peak seasons like summer. So, to save you some penny, make sure to book your tickets early.
  3. Get Everyone on Board with the Destination – Include something on the itinerary for each member of the family. For example, if you are traveling to Cebu, make time to tour some historical sites for any children who are interested in history. If you have someone who is more adventurous, try exploring some of the region's many waterfalls.
  4. Chart Your Itinerary and Print Back-Ups – Always keep printed copies of airline tickets and other reservations just in case you can’t access them digitally. It is also wise to securely scan and upload a copy of passports or other ID to a cloud for easy access anywhere in the world in case of loss.
  5. Make Lists – Making a list of essentials to keep in your bag and tick off each time you leave can help you avoid forgetting things and stay organized.

SAFETY TIPS

  1. Pack sunscreen AND bug spray – It may be the last thing you think you need, but packing sunscreen and bug spray is essential to protecting your skin while traveling. Sunscreen not only prevents skin cancer and other skin conditions, but you’ll avoid pesky sunburns that can linger well after you get home from your trip. If you’re camping or heading to a heavily wooded area, bug spray is a must-have to avoid bites and rashes from mosquitos, ticks, and other critters.
  2. Pack extra snacks and water – Packing extra snacks and water in case of a flat tire, endless traffic, or other delays will come in handy on a hot summer day. Put water bottles in the car to stay hydrated, and pack healthy snacks like granola bars and nuts (if no allergy) to keep the whole family energized.
  3. Don’t forget a first aid kit – Include first-aid supplies such as bandages, antihistamines, antibacterial ointment, and pain relievers in case of bug bites, cuts, or headaches. Don't forget to bring any prescription medications that your family uses too.
  4. Keep important phone numbers on hand – If you’re traveling to a new place this summer, it’s important to be aware of how to access convenient medical care at your location in case of emergency. Jot down phone numbers and addresses of the closest urgent care and emergency room.
  5. Get travel insurance – Travel insurance is recommended whenever you travel both local and abroad. Travel insurance is great for compensating you for canceled or delayed flights, lost baggage, or even lost and stolen items. It also covers medical expenses, trip cancellation, and accidents.
