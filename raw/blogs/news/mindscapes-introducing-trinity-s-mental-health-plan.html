Mindscapes: Introducing Trinity’s Mental Health Plan

November 5, 2024

Mental health is one of the stigmatized issues faced by Philippine society, which can lead to poor attention and resources in providing mental healthcare. However, the Republic Act 11036, or the Mental Health Act of 2018, spotlighted the need to prioritize one’s mental well-being. The law ensures that every Filipino is entitled to have mental healthcare, free from discrimination.

In line with the implementation of the said law, Trinity is determined to provide high-quality, accessible, and innovative solutions that foster mental well-being in the workplace. This introduces Mindscapes, a mental health-focused program that improves a productive work environment.

 

Why is it necessary to have a mental health plan?

Being an advocate for mental health, Trinity listed a few reasons why having a mental health plan can make a positive impact within your workplace.

 

It promotes openness in the workplace.

Supporting mental health guarantees dependability within the organization. This leads to having a strong relationship with colleagues and encouraging open communication within the team. It can also boost resiliency among team members, creating a collective and productive environment where everyone can thrive together.

 

It manages stress.

It is undeniable that stress is part of daily work, and one of the objectives of a mental health plan is to enlighten employees to manage their stress, whether it stems from personal or work-related issues.  This ensures that employees can cope with new challenges present while working productively. Additionally, employees may acknowledge their right to work-life balance with the appropriate coping mechanisms for stress.

 

It provides support for employees.

With the said plan, it helps the employees to get the support that they need with mental health concerns. They can book a session with a licensed counselor or a life coach. This allows them to acquire professional help whenever necessary.

 

It raises mental health awareness.

As mental health is still a stigma in society, it is important to know it, specifically how employees can effectively manage their mental well-being. This is not only limited to work but also to personal status. It is important to prioritize one’s mental health on both organizational and personal levels.

 

COVERAGE OF MINDSCAPES

Mindscapes believes that everyone deserves access to better mental healthcare. It recognizes employee’s ability to cope and manage stress through its outstanding and comprehensive benefits.  This program can be integrated with the client’s existing HMO with Trinity or can also be offered as a standalone service to employees and their dependents. 

 

Expanded Coverage

 

Mind Check

This service covers mental health assessment/screening using different psychological tests such as the Perceived Stress Scale, Depression Scale, and Anxiety Scale. These screenings are available for the first month of the contract, with members having one-time access.

 

24/7 Chat Support

The 24/7 Chat Support service includes an online messaging platform where members can consult with Trained Mental Health Associates (MHAs). This service acts as a counseling triage system, where members are provided with initial intervention based on their mental health concerns. Members will also be given unlimited sessions per year.

 

24/7 MS Helpline

Along with the Chat Support, members will have an online Call Support service with Trained Mental Health Responders (MHRs). This helpline also serves as a counseling triage system where members can have unlimited sessions per year.

 

Mind Care

 

The Mind Care service provides counseling sessions with a Licensed Counselor or Life Coach. Members will have one session per year, with 24/7 online booking. In which sessions may last up to 45 to 60 minutes in duration.

 

Free Services

Included in the Mindscapes platform, members will also have one-year access to Mental Health Assessment Tools, where they can acquire analytical tools for stress, anxiety, and depression, and Mental Health Literacy, where they are provided with one-year access to various mental health literacy tools, such as publications, short videos, and posters.

 

These coverages can benefit the employees as they give knowledge about their mental health. With this, they will be able to manage their stress as it also supports employees in their well-being. 

Trinity is committed to raising Mental Health awareness. We believe that supporting one’s mental health is not only a standard, but it is a necessity in the workplace. Mindscapes ensures a healthy and supportive environment within your organization as it offers comprehensive and scalable coverages.
