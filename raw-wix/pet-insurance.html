Pet Insurance

​

Enjoy playtime with your pets without the worries. Like us hoomans, your pets are prone to sickness and accidents, so the best way to ensure their safety and your financial security is to provide them maximum protection coverage with Trinity Pet Insurance.

Trinity Pet Insurance is a comprehensive medical reimbursement insurance designed for cats and dogs, with an extension of coverage for pet owners.

​

Lost Pet Advertising & Reward: Pays for the cost of advertising or reward in the recovery of the missing insured pet.

Pet Eligibility:

  • 5 months to 7 years old

  • Pure or Mixed Breed (up to 3 mixes only) 

  • Resident of the Philippines

  • Preferably with Microchip (Not required)

  • Dog/Cat must be House Pets only

  • Complete and updated vaccination

Pet Owner Eligibility:

  • 18-75 years old

  • Resident of the Philippines
